Transmission Log · Entry 1943

We have been watching for a long time.

Three suns. No calendar. No mercy.

We do not send this signal lightly. Every world we have catalogued has been discarded for one reason or another. This one, we did not discard.

What follows is the record of why.

Chaotic Era

Three Suns, No Rules

Trisolaris orbits three stars, not one. Their combined gravity is unpredictable — sometimes the planet drifts through a calm, single-sun summer, and sometimes all three converge and the surface boils or freezes within a season.

There is no way to build a calendar. There is no way to plan a harvest, a childhood, a civilization. Whole societies have been dehydrated into spores and rehydrated centuries later, hoping to wake up somewhere survivable.

Stable Era

Occasionally, the suns align into a rhythm long enough to rebuild. It never lasts. It has never once lasted.

Deployment Record · Sophon Class

A Proton, Unfolded

We cannot reach Sol-3 in time to matter. So we send something smaller: a proton, etched with circuitry across nine dimensions, folded down to a point and threaded through the void at the only speed that could arrive before you do.

Once it unfolds, it listens to everything. It blinds your instruments the instant they look too closely at the truth.
